# 03 · Business question matrix

Use the simulated ledger to explain decisions and demonstrate sensitivity. Feasibility refers to the supplied snapshots. **Available** means the descriptive measure can be reproduced. **Conditional** means the answer depends on a stated proxy, reconciliation or sufficient cohort size. **Unsupported** means the outcome, exposure or history required to answer is absent. Priority P0 protects reporting or core closeout decisions, P1 improves operating decisions, and P2 covers planning or future data acquisition.

Source shorthand in the matrix refers to the full table names in [01_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md](01_SOURCE_INVENTORY.md). Closeout, Cattle, Lot, Pen, Feed, Scale and Health are the workbook facts and dimensions. Flat is the reconciled animal-level projection. CAD is nominal Canadian dollars. Compare groups with counts, denominators, dates and uncertainty. Cohorts overlap on factors such as breed, origin, starting weight, season and shelter. Associations cannot establish intervention effects.
